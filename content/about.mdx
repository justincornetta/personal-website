That's what pulled me into psychedelic medicine, bitcoin, energy, and technology/AI, where I invest my time, energy, and personal capital.

Shaped by a diverse set of experiences and skills, I'd describe myself as a generalist. For a long time, that felt like a disadvantage compared to specialists. But as AI and technology have accelerated, I've come to see that generalists are well equipped to navigate new environments and complexity. That range has helped me build a versatile foundation and a sharper lens for why the world is the way it is, where it's heading, and how to position accordingly. ## Healthcare

The early part of my career has been rooted in healthcare operations. In college, I pivoted from a pre-med path to healthcare management, bringing together my interests in medicine, healthcare, and business. That shift led me to Teladoc Health, the largest virtual-care company in the world.

Over this period, I've grown significantly, supported by mentors and shaped by diverse experience across client experience & operations, program management, clinical operations, patient experience, and performance operations. I executed multiple C-suite-level initiatives, including implementing new capabilities in [PRISM](https://www.teladochealth.com/newsroom/press/teladoc-health-connects-digital-community-providers-to-deliver-next-generation-of-virtually-integrated-care), Teladoc's proprietary care delivery platform, and building a new performance operations function from the ground up. My work at Teladoc has directly led to $4.5M+ in annual cost savings, $1.3M+ in incremental revenue, $10M+ client contract renewal + $20M+ upsell, and new commercial opportunities. The breadth of my work and the people I've learned from have been an immense foundation as I've begun my career, for which I am grateful.

## Psychedelic medicine

Driven by curiosity and my neuroscience background at Penn, I've been drawn to the science of psychedelic medicine since 2021. In a neuroscience class on hallucinogens, including psilocybin and LSD, I first realized these "drugs" could have immense potential for healing and for advancing science. I became fascinated by the contrast between the positive results from recent clinical literature and my prior assumptions, shaped by mainstream perception.

Throughout my time at Penn, I continued learning to better understand the roots of that disconnect (see ["Medicine in History (Health and Societies) Final Project - 2021"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJ1BDSdRUuc)) and the broader science behind psychedelic medicine. A major inflection point was [hearing personal healing stories directly from clinical trial participants](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ccD2FlAWYdE) during a seminar series at Penn, which became my "why" and motivation to stay involved in this industry.

Without an outlet to share this passion, I initially felt lost. That's what led me to restart a psychedelic society during my graduate studies at Rutgers University (see "[RU Psyched Club Manual](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NF2iuvrhoLAkkLj_odi6kYI9i_AG8Q6w/view?usp=drive_link)") and to expand the mission on a global scale through the [Intercollegiate Psychedelics Network](https://www.intercollegiatepsychedelics.net/). Within a year at Rutgers, we recruited a team of 7 officers and 250+ members, and organized 9 educational events to advance responsible psychedelic discourse and community at Rutgers University. As a result, we were invited to join the New Jersey Psilocybin Coalition to support advocacy of [NJ S2283: Psilocybin Behavioral Health Access and Services Act](https://www.njleg.state.nj.us/bill-search/2024/S2283/bill-text?f=S2500&n=2283_R1). At IPN, we've grown to 2,100+ members across 70+ countries, supporting students' professional growth and career development through a community to connect and hundreds of hours of free programming.

Today, I'm most passionate about expanding my reach in this space as psychedelic medicine enters the mainstream. I care about the path from clinical validation to access, and I look forward to opportunities to work with individuals and companies to deliver psychedelic therapy safely and efficiently at scale.

## Investing & markets: Bitcoin, Technology/AI, Energy

In my spare time, I write and share market research at @jmjcapital across individual companies, macroeconomics, Bitcoin, energy, technology, and psychedelic medicine. I enjoy understanding businesses from the inside out and forming a view before the consensus does.

JMJ Capital started in 2021 as a joke among a few friends (JMJ stands for Justin, Mason, Jamie). As a neuroscience major, I didn't have a traditional educational background in finance, markets, or economics, so I leaned on friends, online communities, podcasts, books, and LLMs to build my understanding.

Today, I share this passion with a broader group of friends and an online community on X. The industries I research are rooted in my core philosophy of investing in areas with asymmetry.

- **Bitcoin:** Bitcoin is a savings technology the world has never had. Every prior form of money, from rai stones to metals to fiat, fails on scarcity, portability, and/or decentralization, which is exactly how governments debase currency and quietly erode people's savings, from [coin clipping during the Roman Empire](https://onrampbitcoin.com/knowledge-center/what-is-coin-clipping-the-original-form-of-currency-debasement) to [1930s US gold seizures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_Order_6102) to [Venezuela's hyperinflation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XbKQ9_RFIJc) to [today's cost-of-living crisis](https://wtfhappenedin1971.com/). Bitcoin is the first money that is scarce, divisible, portable, and decentralized at once, which gives individuals real protection from government overreach and monetary debasement. Most people dismiss it as magic internet money because they have never questioned the money they inherited.
- **Energy:** We are in the middle of an energy transformation, accelerated by electrification and AI's rising power demand. Headlines fixate on nuclear, gas, and coal, but [solar, wind, and battery storage keep winning new grid capacity because they are accessible and cheap today](https://x.com/jmjcapital/status/1939073824684941636/photo/1) and only getting cheaper every year, [driven by steep learning curves](https://x.com/jmjcapital/status/1743820001566400687?s=20). I pay closest attention to battery storage: it solves the intermittency of renewables and [lets existing gas and nuclear run at full capacity while the battery manages the supply and demand imbalance](https://x.com/jmjcapital/status/1981328994394845293?s=20). A large market solving a persistent problem: meeting today's accelerating energy demand.
- **AI / Technology:** Many still call AI a bubble like the 2001 dot-com era, but the loudest skeptics usually are not daily users. Using Claude Opus 4.5 and OpenClaw after their release made it obvious to me that we are in the early innings of a real technological shift. Pairing that with what I know about energy, especially the physical constraints and long lead times to add power to the grid, pointed me to the companies with the resources to build out AI infrastructure. [That thesis led me to companies like IREN, CIFR, and WULF in 2024.](https://x.com/jmjcapital/status/1931367978353688635)

Beyond building wealth, investing has sharpened how I see the world and how companies operate. It has taught me patience, discipline, risk management, and critical thinking, lessons that carry into everything else I do.
