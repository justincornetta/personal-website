## About this article

FDA approval is one step in bringing a medicine to patients. The opening article of *Beyond Approval* introduces the next question: what has to happen for an approved treatment to become widely used?

Using Spravato as an operating precedent, the article examines the delivery ecosystem around a treatment: patient awareness and referrals, treatment-center capacity, insurance coverage, clinical workflows, and the practical demands on patients.

It also introduces the research series and the questions that later chapters will explore: where adoption breaks down, how those constraints differ across products, and what determines which psychedelic medicines can scale commercially.
