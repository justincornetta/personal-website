## About this article

The FDA’s September 2026 public hearing offered little direct policy signal, but it surfaced four implementation challenges that could shape the commercial rollout of future psychedelic medicines: provider education, product labels and REMS, care coordination and reimbursement, and standardized data collection.

This field note considers how those constraints could affect referrals, treatment-center capacity, insurance coverage, and the number of patients who ultimately reach treatment.
