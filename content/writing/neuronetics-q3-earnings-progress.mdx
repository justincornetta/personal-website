## About this article

Neuronetics reported Q3 results on November 4, showing its first quarter of positive GAAP operating income since its 2018 IPO. Both of its businesses contributed: NeuroStar, which makes TMS devices, and Greenbrook, its network of 93 interventional psychiatry clinics.

This field note looks at:

- What drove the improvement in each business
- How much of it may last
- Where future growth could come from

For Beyond Approval, the results offer a useful look at the economics of running the treatment centers that will eventually deliver FDA-approved psychedelic medicines.
