## Problem

IPN's operating data lived across too many places. Membership, email, Discord, social media, website analytics, Eventbrite, Zoom, and donation activity each had their own reporting surface. That made it hard for leadership to see organizational health, understand what was working, or make timely decisions.

The problem was not a lack of data. It was the absence of a practical operating dashboard that could turn fragmented data into a shared management view.

## Objective

The objective was to create a centralized dashboard for IPN leadership that could show growth, engagement, event performance, and marketing funnel health in one place. The dashboard needed to start with whatever data was available, then evolve toward automated API pulls and recurring summaries.

<MetricStrip variant="dashboard" />

## Solution

The dashboard was designed as an operations layer over IPN's scattered tool stack. It tracks KPI cards, growth trends, engagement performance, source status, funnel indicators, and event-level reporting.

The system evolved from a prototype and spreadsheet tracker into a more automated pipeline. Mailchimp, Discord, Instagram, Facebook, GA4, Squarespace, Zoom, and Eventbrite all became part of the data-source plan, with manual templates filling the gaps where APIs were not yet practical.

## How I contributed

My contribution was shaping the dashboard as a management tool rather than a vanity reporting page. The goal was to help IPN answer practical questions: Are members growing? Where are people engaging? Which channels are working? Which event surfaces are converting? What should the board pay attention to this week?

I also helped frame the phased rollout: prototype first, semi-automated reporting next, and then a fuller daily refresh pipeline with weekly summaries and recommendations.

## Outcome

The dashboard became a clearer operating system for IPN's growth and engagement data. It connected multiple data sources, created a single shared view of performance, and gave leadership a more concrete way to discuss member engagement, website activity, event performance, and marketing channels.

The important lesson was that analytics work becomes more valuable when it is tied to recurring operating decisions. The dashboard was not just a place to view numbers. It was a foundation for a better management rhythm.
