## Problem

IPN had a strong mission and growing member base, but the member experience was spread across disconnected surfaces: forms, Discord, Zoom, Mailchimp, event links, and the public website. That made it harder for new members to understand where to go, how to engage, and what IPN could help them do next.

The core issue was not simply "we need a portal." It was that IPN needed a clearer operating model for member onboarding, discovery, resources, events, and future career support.

## Objective

The objective was to define and begin shipping a member portal that could become the central home for IPN members while staying realistic for a volunteer-led nonprofit. The project needed to balance cost, build time, long-term maintenance, and the team's actual capacity.

<MetricStrip variant="portal" />

## Solution

I helped frame the portal around a practical v1 scope: member identity, profiles, resources, events, and a path toward future career and community workflows. The recommended stack became a custom Next.js application on Vercel with Supabase for auth and data, plus Mailchimp as the existing member communication spine.

That recommendation came after comparing multiple paths: continuing an older Firebase and Expo effort, building a new Supabase-based app, or using hosted community platforms like Circle or Mighty Networks. The decision favored a custom build because it preserved flexibility, kept recurring costs low, and created a more rewarding AI-assisted build path for IPN.

## How I contributed

My contribution was primarily strategic and operational. I helped translate a broad member-experience problem into a platform decision, requirements structure, launch sequence, and risk-managed build path.

I worked through the tradeoffs that actually matter for a nonprofit technology project: who can maintain it, what can ship by a real deadline, what needs to be deferred, and where custom software is worth the added complexity.

## Outcome

The result was a clear product direction and a live greenfield build path for the IPN Member Portal. More importantly, the project established a stronger operating frame for IPN's member experience: start with the workflows that create clarity for members, then expand into richer community and career features once the foundation is stable.
